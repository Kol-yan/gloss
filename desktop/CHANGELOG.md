# Changelog

Changes to the gloss desktop app.

## 1.0.0 — First public release

### Features

* **Menu-bar app for macOS.** Runs on macOS 11 or later, on Apple silicon and
  Intel Macs.
* **Check selected text.** Select text in any app and press **⌃⌥G**. A panel
  shows the suggestions; tick the ones you want and choose **Apply** (or
  **⌘↩**) to replace your selection, or **Copy** to take the corrected text.
* **Check as you type.** While you type in another app, a small badge next to
  the field shows how many fixes gloss found. Click it to review them and apply
  them in place. It can be switched off in Settings.
* **Push-to-talk dictation.** Hold the right Option key, speak and let go: the
  cleaned-up text appears where you are typing, with your own words kept. A
  quick tap starts hands-free recording; tap again to stop. Recordings stop at
  60 seconds and are never saved to disk.
* **Your clipboard is kept.** When gloss has to copy or paste to do its work,
  it puts your previous clipboard back afterwards.
* **Settings.** Change both shortcuts, see whether Accessibility and microphone
  access are granted, and set a self-hosted server under **Advanced**.
* **No sign-in.** The app creates its own guest workspace on first launch and
  keeps it between launches.
