## Description

```text
gloss helps you write English you can stand behind, in the places where you already write.

Check as you type
Write in any text field on any website: an email, a comment, a form, a chat message. When you pause, gloss looks at what you wrote and underlines what needs attention. Red underlines are mistakes. Blue underlines are style notes. Hover or click an underline to see the fix and a short reason, then choose Apply or Dismiss. Apply edits the text the way typing would, so Ctrl+Z undoes it.

Save the words you want to learn
Select a word or a short phrase on any page, right-click, and choose Add to My words. You can add a meaning in your own words right away. Switch on Highlight My words in the toolbar popup to see your saved words marked on the page you are reading; hover one to see its meaning.

Practise on gloss.li
My words is part of your gloss workspace at gloss.li, where you can practise using your words in short writing and speaking tasks.

No sign-in
gloss.li starts a free guest workspace for you the first time you open it, and the extension uses it. The free workspace includes a daily number of writing checks; the popup shows how many are left today.

You stay in control
- Turn checks off for one site in the toolbar popup, or for all sites in the settings.
- Password fields, fields for names, emails, card numbers and one-time codes, and read-only fields are never checked.
- Highlighting runs only after you switch it on for a page, and the page text never leaves your browser.
- No page address or page title is sent anywhere.

What gloss sends
The extension talks only to the gloss server (gloss.li by default, or a server you host yourself). It sends the text of the field you are typing in while checks are on for that site, the words you add, and your Apply or Dismiss choices. The server uses AI models to find suggestions. Privacy policy: https://gloss.li/privacy

gloss is open source under the AGPL: https://github.com/Kol-yan/gloss
```

## Single purpose (Privacy practices tab)

```text
gloss helps people write better English on the web. It checks the English text a user types in web page text fields and offers corrections in place, and it lets the user save English words and phrases they want to learn to their gloss word list and see those words marked on pages they read.
```
