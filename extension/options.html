Signing in

The extension uses your gloss session in this browser. Open your invite link once in this browser, then come back here and check the connection.

Writing checks

After you pause typing, the text of the field you are writing in is sent to your gloss server for a cloud check. Each check counts toward your daily allowance. Password and other structured fields are never checked.

Sites where checks are off

    None. Use “Off on this site” in the toolbar popup to add one.

    This extension stores only your server address, whether writing checks are on, and the sites where you turned them off. Saved words travel to your server with fetch requests; nothing is put in page addresses or browser history. “Check with gloss” still opens the editor with the selection in the address fragment.
